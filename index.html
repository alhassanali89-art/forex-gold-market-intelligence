How the prototype analyses candles

It calculates EMA 20/50/200, RSI 14, MACD direction and ATR 14 from the uploaded close/high/low values. Its simple rules produce an educational directional indication, not a validated trading strategy. It does not check spread, slippage, execution quality, economic events or higher-timeframe confirmation.
